FAQ

Q: Can I get a refund for my order?

A: Yes. We offer refunds on every item except coins containing gold or silver due to market price fluctuations. Please visit our Refund Policy for a full explanation.

Q: How long until I get my item?

A: We ship orders as quickly as possible. However, carrier delays, distance from our facility, holidays, natural disasters, or a backlog on our end may affect delivery times.

Q: What payment methods do you offer?

A: As a small business, we currently accept card payments only. We plan to add more payment options in the future.

Q: Can I cancel an order?

A: You may cancel an order only before we send your shipping confirmation email. To cancel, please contact us through email or our contact form. Canceled orders may incur processing fees. Please visit our Refund Policy for details.

Q: Do you offer international shipping?

A: We do not currently offer international shipping due to our size as a small business. We plan to add this option in the future.

Q: What should I do if I receive a damaged product?

A: If your item arrives damaged, please submit a refund request form. We may issue a refund or send a replacement free of charge if the item is in stock.

Q: How long does it take to get my refund?

A: After you submit a refund request form, we take up to 5 business days to review it. We will then contact you with the next steps. Please refer to our Refund Policy for a complete explanation.

Q: Are your coins authentic?

A: We thoroughly inspect all purchased and returned coins for authenticity. If you believe you received a counterfeit item, please submit a refund request form.

Q: Do you restock sold-out items?

A: Our inventory depends on what we acquire. Common coins are more likely to be restocked, while rare coins are usually not restocked.

Q: How can I ask you any questions?

A: You can contact us anytime by filling out our contact form.

Q: What are your support hours and how quickly do you respond?

A: You may send a message at any time. We do not operate on fixed business hours. All support is handled online, and most inquiries are answered within 1-2 business days. Response times may be longer during periods of high volume.
