Refund Eligibility

Items may only be refunded if one of the following conditions applies:

  • The customer received the wrong item
  • The item was lost in transit and remains unlocatable after a 14-day carrier investigation window following a lost‑package declaration
  • Order cancellation only before shipping confirmation.
  • The item was damaged upon delivery (requires photo proof)

There will be no refund for the following:

  • The item is not to the customer's liking
  • Any disagreement regarding the coin's grade or condition (grading is subjective and non-refundable)
  • No refund for any silver or gold coins
  • If there is evidence of any fraudulent activity

All refunds will have a mandatory 5 day reviewing window. An email will be sent within 5 days of the refund request containing refund status and next steps.

Refunds will only be issued to the original payment method.

Refund Request Window

Customers have the following timeframes for requesting refunds:

  • Shipping Damage: Must be reported within 48 hours of delivery (photo proof required)
  • General Refund Requests: Must be submitted within 7 days of delivery for any eligible reason stated above
  • Precious Metals: There will be absolutely no refund for silver or gold coins. This overrides any other conditions that would normally warrant a refund.

Refund Condition Requirements

All items must meet all of the following conditions:

  • The item must be returned in its original container and in the same condition as it was shipped
  • The item cannot be altered in any way. This includes, but is not limited to: chemical reactions, cleaning, scratching, or dents
  • The coin cannot be swapped. All returned coins will be tested when we receive them to ensure authenticity
  • If the coin was shipped in a slab, the slab certification numbers must match when we receive it
  • All accessories shipped with the coin must be returned. This includes, but is not limited to: flips, capsules, paper documents, and protective elements

Non-Refundable Items

The following items can never be refunded nor returned:

  • Any coin containing silver and/or gold of any amount. This overrides any other rule.
  • Items with damage or defects listed in the product description
  • Any item that was discounted or on sale at the time of purchase
  • Any mystery or grab bag item cannot be refunded. This also includes any item where condition and wear is not guaranteed and is explicitly listed in the product description
  • A refund will not be given if any part of the packaging is missing, including but not limited to: protective elements, documents, flips, and capsules.
  • Any item that is damaged by the customer is not eligible for refunds

Return Shipping Policy

The following rules apply to all return shipping and refund requests:

  • Customer-Paid Return Shipping: For all eligible refunds, the customer is responsible for paying return shipping costs.
  • Seller Error: If the wrong item was shipped or there was an error in the order caused by us, we will cover the cost of return shipping.
  • Shipping Damage: Any damage during shipping must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. Photo proof of the item and packaging is required, and the customer must keep all packaging materials until the claim is resolved.
  • Lost Packages: Lost package claims will only be processed after a carrier investigation and confirmation of loss. Refunds for lost packages are not issued immediately and depend on the carrier's final decision.
  • Non-Refundable Shipping Costs: Original shipping charges are non-refundable. Return shipping costs are also non-refundable unless the return is due to a confirmed error on our part.
  • Trackable Return Shipping: Customers must use a trackable shipping method for all returns and retain proof of shipment. We are not responsible for returns that are lost or damaged in transit.
  • Proper Packaging: Customers are responsible for safely packaging returned items. Coins, slabs, and accessories must be securely protected to prevent damage during return shipping.
  • Discrepancies: If the returned item does not match the coin originally shipped — including differences in condition, authenticity, packaging, or slab certification — only a partial refund of up to 50% of the purchase price will be offered.

Refund Processing Time

The following timeframes always apply and no exceptions are ever granted:

  • All refunds have a maximum 5 business day review period following the refund claim
  • Once we have successfully received an item, we will take up to 7 business days to review and inspect the item before processing the refund
  • We reserve the right to refuse refunds if we suspect any fraudulent activity
  • Refunds are always sent to the original payment method
  • Refunds are not instant and depend on banks, apps, holidays, and any other factors affecting payment time
  • We offer up to 50% of the original purchase price if a discrepancy is found during inspection
  • Email confirmations and updates will be sent to ensure smoothness of the refund process
