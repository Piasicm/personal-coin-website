Refund Eligibility

Items may only be refunded if one of the following conditions applies:

  • The customer received the wrong item. We will provide a prepaid return shipping label or reimburse reasonable return shipping costs.
  • The item was lost in transit and remains unlocatable after a 14-day carrier investigation window following a lost‑package declaration.
  • Order cancellation only before shipping confirmation.
  • The item was damaged upon delivery (requires photo proof).

There will be no refund for the following:

  • The item is not to the customer's liking.
  • Any disagreement regarding the coin's grade or condition, unless the item was materially misrepresented in the product listing. Coin grading is subjective and is otherwise non-refundable.
  • Any coin containing any amount of silver and/or gold. Due to precious metal market volatility, all precious metal sales are final.
  • We reserve the right to deny refunds where we reasonably suspect fraud, abuse of our refund policy, or attempts to return an item other than the one originally shipped.

All refunds will have a mandatory 5‑day reviewing window. An email will be sent within 5 days of the refund request containing refund status and next steps.

Refunds will only be issued to the original payment method.

Refund Request Window

Delivery time is determined by the carrier's official delivery confirmation.

Customers have the following timeframes for requesting refunds:

  • Shipping Damage: Must be reported within 48 hours of delivery (photo proof required).
  • General Refund Requests: Must be submitted within 7 days of delivery for any eligible reason stated above.

Refund Condition Requirements

All items must meet all of the following conditions:

  • The item must be returned in its original container and in the same condition as it was shipped.
  • The item cannot be altered in any way. This includes, but is not limited to: chemical reactions, cleaning, scratching, or dents.
  • The coin cannot be swapped. All returned coins will be tested when we receive them to ensure authenticity.
  • If the coin was shipped in a slab, the slab certification numbers must match when we receive it.
  • All accessories shipped with the coin must be returned. This includes, but is not limited to: flips, capsules, paper documents, and protective elements.
  • Minor cosmetic damage to outer shipping materials alone will not automatically void a refund, provided all required contents are returned.

Non-Refundable Items

The following items can never be refunded nor returned:

  • Any coin containing any amount of silver and/or gold. Due to precious metal market volatility, all precious metal sales are final.
  • Items with damage or defects listed in the product description.
  • Any item that was discounted or on sale at the time of purchase.
  • Any mystery or grab bag item cannot be refunded. This includes any item where condition and wear is not guaranteed and is explicitly listed in the product description.
  • A refund will not be given if any part of the packaging is missing, including but not limited to: protective elements, documents, flips, and capsules.
  • Any item that is damaged by the customer is not eligible for refunds.

Return Shipping Policy

The following rules apply to all return shipping and refund requests:

  • Customer-Paid Return Shipping: For all eligible refunds, the customer is responsible for paying return shipping costs.
  • Seller Error: If we shipped the wrong item or made an error with your order, we will provide a prepaid return shipping label or reimburse reasonable return shipping costs.
  • Shipping Damage: Any damage during shipping must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. Photo proof of the item and packaging is required, and the customer must keep all packaging materials until the claim is resolved.
  • Lost Packages: Lost package claims will only be processed after a carrier investigation and confirmation of loss.
  • Non-Refundable Shipping Costs: Original shipping charges are non-refundable. Return shipping costs are also non-refundable unless the return is due to a confirmed error on our part.
  • Trackable Return Shipping: Customers must use a trackable shipping method for all returns and retain proof of shipment.
  • Proper Packaging: Customers are responsible for safely packaging returned items.
  • Discrepancies: If the returned item differs from the item originally shipped due to customer actions—including altered condition, missing accessories, swapped coins, authenticity concerns, or mismatched slab certification numbers—we may deny the refund entirely or issue a partial refund of up to 50% of the purchase price at our sole discretion.
  • Return Authorization: All returns must be approved through our refund request process before being shipped. Unauthorized returns may be refused.
  • Return Address: Returns must only be shipped to the return address provided during the refund approval process.
  • Unclaimed Packages: Orders returned to us because they were refused or left unclaimed may be subject to additional shipping charges before being reshipped.

Refund Processing Time

The following timeframes always apply and no exceptions are ever granted:

  • All refunds have a maximum 5 business day review period following the refund claim.
  • Once we have successfully received an item, we will take up to 7 business days to review and inspect the item before processing the refund.
  • Submitting a payment dispute or chargeback while a refund request is under review may suspend or delay the refund process until the dispute has been resolved.
  • Refunds are always sent to the original payment method.
  • Refunds are not instant and depend on banks, apps, holidays, and other factors affecting payment time.
  • Email confirmations and updates will be sent to ensure smoothness of the refund process.

Restock Fees

The following fees and tiers are based on the value of returned items. The fee will automatically be deducted from the refunded amount.

  • Value $0-$10: Flat $1 fee
  • Value $10.01-$100: 5% fee + $3 flat fee
  • Value $100.01-$200: 5% fee + $5 flat fee
  • Value $200.01+: 5% fee

Additional per-item fees apply for bulk returns:

  • Item count 1-10: No per item fees
  • Item count 11-20: $0.07 per item
  • Item count 21-50: $0.10 per item
  • Item count 51-100: $0.12 per item
  • Item count 101+: $0.15 per item

Restocking fees do not apply when the return is the result of our error, including shipping the wrong item or verified shipping damage.

The total fees will never exceed 25% of the total item's value. In cases where it would exceed this amount, fees will be capped at 25%.
