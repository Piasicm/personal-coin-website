About Us

I've collected since I was 6 years old. My first ever coin was a special Brilliant Uncirculated straight from the mint gifted to our whole class from the mayor. Ever since, I have been collecting coins, picking up coins from pocket change, finding coins people dropped, buying coins and much more.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver coins at the lowest possible price. We also offer high buyback prices. We try to make coin collecting like it should be-as close to the market value as possible.

What we offer

We offer common circulation coins, world silver coins as well as junk silver, high grade coins, rare coins, and much more. We mostly do raw coins but also sometimes do slabbed coins. Our coin selection is largely dependent on what people offer us, along with what we can buy. So no guarantees on what we store! We may have what you are looking for one day, but once someone buys it, chances are that it is not available for some time.
